Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 31, 2026

  • Advertising identifiers used for rewarded advertisements.

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide access to courses and learning features.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

Changes to this Policy
